# AOI v0.5 unpublished internal adoption

The public `v0.5.0a1` route and completion boundary in the canonical
[v0.5 implementation and release plan](v0.5-plan.md) remain unchanged. One
bounded local exception may precede public publication for maintainer-owned
internal adoption. It is not a release, promotion, public readback, Company OS
READY result, or evidence that the v0.5 completion boundary passed. It may not
create or move a tag, publish to GitHub or PyPI, upload a release artifact, or
substitute local evidence for any public or provider-live gate.

The exception is fail-closed and ordered:

1. This policy change itself must receive independent exact-byte review, normal
   non-admin Git delivery, and fresh successful `test.yml` plus `docs.yml`
   readback on the same exact merged `main` commit.
2. A clean external worktree at that commit must create exactly one reviewed
   `reviewed_local_install_bundle` with
   `proof_scope=exact_local_wheel_install_only`. The bundle, wheel, sdist,
   source manifest, inventory, rehearsal, review, and seal remain local and
   content-addressed. Caller-supplied test context is not source-to-wheel
   derivation proof.
3. The exact wheel must be installed offline in a new side-by-side AOI-only
   environment while frozen N-1 and rollback assets remain available. Package,
   installed-metadata, synthetic Company OS, Supervisor, IC-Pack, IC-RAG,
   hook-receipt rotation, and archive canaries must pass on disposable state.
   The synthetic Company OS canary is not provider-live Company OS READY.
4. Internal rehearsal and distribution readback may verify only those local
   content-addressed artifacts. They grant no public release or promotion
   status.
5. Before touching an existing project, every old writer must be proven
   quiescent; a complete repo-external backup and non-live rollback rehearsal
   must verify; and a digest-bound migration preview must retain the exact
   candidate proof, preimages, old environment, and rollback assets. The
   side-by-side migration may apply once only while those identities remain
   exact, followed by hooks, doctor, task, Chief, handoff, and Dashboard
   readback. Unknown effect, drift, failed rollback, or non-quiescence stops the
   route without retry or relaunch.
6. Only after that internal migration and readback may a separate AOI-managed
   ARISE read-only dogfood task begin. It may use existing evidence and produce
   a repo-external architecture decision package, but may not modify ARISE
   source, RTL, docs, AOI project files, or Git, and may not launch VCS, EDA, or
   GPU work. It is internal dogfood evidence only and cannot be promoted into
   `v0.5.0a1` release evidence.

This exception does not satisfy any public acceptance target. Public ARISE
adoption still follows public readback, and public v0.5 completion still
requires every item in the canonical completion boundary.
